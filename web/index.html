Briefing

How it works

  1. 01

    Get your link

    An admin starts your check in Discord, or you use /handcam join. The bot sends you a private, one-time link.

  2. 02

    Set up the camera

    Open the link on a second phone, tablet or laptop webcam. Fit your whole game screen inside the brackets and tap Go live.

  3. 03

    Play your match

    Play as normal. If your screen slips out of view, the camera phone tells you out loud how to fix it.

Rules of engagement

What gets checked

Whole screen in view

Your entire game screen stays inside the camera for the full match. Hands on the screen are fine.

No mod menus or ESP

Cheat menus, wallhack boxes, snap lines, aim circles and cheat watermarks are flagged.

A real, live feed

Frozen, looped or pre-recorded video is detected. The camera has to show what's really happening.

Stay connected

Keep the cam page open until the match ends. It reconnects by itself if your connection drops.

Loadout

Before your match

  • A second device: any phone, tablet or laptop webcam. Not the one you play on.
  • Plugged in and on Wi-Fi if you can.
  • Volume up on the camera phone for voice alerts.
  • Good light, no bright lamp reflecting on your screen.
  • Works in the browser on iPhone, Android and laptops.

Data per 30-minute match

Fair play, both ways

Your privacy, a fair hearing

Only the camera

We see what the camera sees and nothing else. No access to your phone, files or account.

Deleted after 7 days

Recordings are only used to check your match. Clean ones are deleted automatically.

A human decides

The AI only flags. An admin reviews every flag with the clip. No automatic bans.

One link, one device

Your link works once, on one device, and expires. Nobody else can stream as you.

Questions

My screen keeps going out of frame. What do I do?

Move the camera back or a little further away until your whole screen fits inside the brackets; they turn green when it's right. The camera phone also tells you out loud what to fix.

Can I use my laptop webcam?

Yes. Open the link in a browser on the laptop and point the webcam at your game screen.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The cam page runs in Safari, Chrome and other modern browsers. Allow camera access when asked.

Can it see my phone's files or messages?

No. It only receives the video from the camera you point at your game screen.

What if I get flagged by mistake?

Every flag goes to a human admin with a snapshot and a short clip. If it's wrong, they mark it as a false positive and nothing happens to you.

My hands cover the screen while I play. Is that a problem?

No. Fingers and thumbs on the screen are normal and expected. Just keep the edges of the screen in view.
